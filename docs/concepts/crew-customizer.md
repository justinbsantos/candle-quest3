# Crew customizer

Bodies are the painted crew only: Bram the bull, Nera the bear, Vesper the fox, Calder the wolf.

Players do not redraw the face. They change equipment.

Slots:
- Lane: Field or Studio. A cut, not a gender.
- Jacket dye.
- Cap or beanie.
- Headphones.
- Tool: chart roll or tablet.
- Flame patch.

Species is free. Extra pieces cost gems. Nothing in a slot changes a fill, a stop, or a coin payout.
